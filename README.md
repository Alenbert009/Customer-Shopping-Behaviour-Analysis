---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Project Objective

In modern retail, profitability relies heavily on understanding consumer buying habits. Identifying which demographics spend the most, how promotions impact cart value, and which products maintain high customer satisfaction allows businesses to optimize their inventory, marketing, and loyalty initiatives.

This enterprise-grade portfolio project provides a **360-degree look at retail commerce behavior**. By combining rigorous data engineering and ETL processes in **Python**, professional database auditing via **SQL**, and an interactive executive presentation layer in **Power BI**, the project turns raw operational records into actionable business strategies.
